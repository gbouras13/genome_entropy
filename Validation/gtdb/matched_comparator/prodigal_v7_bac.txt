SHADOW COINCIDENCE BY FRAME CLASS -- why the pooled background is wrong
==============================================================================
  frame class                          n   coincide     rate
  opposite strand                359,596     35,291    9.81%
  same strand, frameshift        162,933     71,888   44.12%

  A same-frame shadow coinciding with a Prodigal call is Prodigal
  agreeing with GenBank about a gene that IS annotated. It says
  nothing about whether Prodigal endorses a spurious ORF, so it does
  not belong in the background for this test.

AGAINST THE CLEAN COMPARATOR (antisense and frameshift shadows only)
==============================================================================
  clean shadows            : 522,529 of 522,529
  candidate coincidence    : 47.42%
  clean shadow coincidence : 20.51%
  excess                   : 26.91% (95% CI 26.74 - 27.09)
  implied candidates independently called: 140,845 of 523,346
  enrichment over background: 2.3x

  HOW INDEPENDENT IS THIS, HONESTLY
  Independent of GenBank annotation and of structural homology --
  Prodigal consults neither. NOT independent of 'looks
  compositionally like coding sequence': Prodigal scores codon
  usage and GC, and our candidates are selected partly on
  protein-like composition, so the two share that prior. The
  clean-shadow comparator is what bounds it: those ORFs are also
  compositionally protein-like and Prodigal still declines them.

  This is the stricter of the two backgrounds where it is higher
  than the clean-shadow rate, because it removes the
  already-committed-call confound. Quote whichever is LOWER as
  the excess -- i.e. take the larger background.

  excess over matched shadows: 26.91% (95% CI 26.74 - 27.09)
  implied candidates independently called by Prodigal: 140,845 of 523,346
  enrichment over background: 2.3x

  (The pooled row above is retained for continuity but the clean
   comparator is the one to quote.)

  Caveat: Prodigal over-calls relative to curated annotation, so its
  agreement is corroboration and not proof. The shadow arm is what
  makes it quantitative -- it measures how often Prodigal endorses an
  ORF that we can independently say is NOT the real gene.
